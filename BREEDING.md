# Chickens breeding reference

Breed resource chickens with chicken food, including seeds. A different-breed pair can produce either parent or one of its listed unlocks; unlocks are not guaranteed. A new breed begins with Growth/Gain/Strength 1/1/1. Breed matching types to improve those stats.

Colored eggs are crafted with an ordinary egg and the corresponding modern dye. This provides a survival route to all 16 color breeds, including those with breeding recipes.
